# Getting Started

Welcome to **SlotFlow**! This guide will help you create your account, sign in securely, complete your profile, and book your first appointment. Follow the steps below to get started in just a few minutes.

## 1. Creating an Account

Creating a SlotFlow account allows you to discover service providers, schedule appointments, manage bookings, and receive real-time updates.

### Steps for creating an account

1. Open the **Sign Up** page.
2. Enter your full name.
3. Provide a valid email address.
4. Create a strong password.
5. Read and accept the **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy**.
6. Click **Create Account**.

Once your account has been created, you'll be asked to verify your email address before accessing all features.

### Tips

- Use an email address you access regularly.
- Create a password containing uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
- Keep your login credentials secure and never share them with anyone.

---

## 2. Logging In

After creating your account and verifying your email, you can sign in to access your dashboard.

### Steps for login

1. Open the **Login** page.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Enter your password.
4. Click **Sign In**.

If you registered using Google, simply select **Continue with Google**.

### Forgot Your Password?

If you can't remember your password:

1. Select **Forgot Password** on the login page.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Follow the password reset instructions sent to your inbox.
4. Create a new password and sign in again.

---

## 3. Verifying Your Email

Email verification helps keep your account secure and ensures you receive important notifications regarding appointments and payments.

### How It Works

After registration:

1. SlotFlow sends a verification email to your registered email address.
2. Open the email.
3. Click the **Verify Email** button or verification link.
4. Your account will be activated immediately.

### Didn't Receive the Email?

If the verification email doesn't arrive:

- Check your Spam or Junk folder.
- Wait a few minutes and refresh your inbox.
- Ensure you entered the correct email address during registration.
- Request a new verification email from the login page if available.

> **Note:** Some platform features may remain unavailable until your email address has been verified.

---

## 4. Completing Your Profile

A complete profile helps providers identify you correctly and makes managing appointments easier.

### Recommended Information

Although only basic account information is required, completing your profile improves your overall experience.

You can update information such as:

- Full name
- Profile photo (optional)
- Phone number (optional)
- Preferred contact information
- Time zone (if applicable)

### Why Complete Your Profile?

A completed profile helps you:

- Receive personalized appointment information.
- Identify your bookings more easily.
- Improve communication with service providers.
- Manage future appointments efficiently.

You can update your profile at any time from your account settings.

---

## 5. Your First Booking

Booking your first appointment only takes a few steps.

### Step 1: Browse Services

Explore available service providers and choose the service that best fits your needs.

You can review:

- Available services
- Provider information
- Available dates and times
- Pricing (when applicable)

---

### Step 2: Select a Time Slot

Choose an available date and time that works for you.

Available slots are updated based on the provider's availability.

---

### Step 3: Review Your Booking

Before confirming your appointment, review:

- Provider
- Selected service
- Appointment date
- Appointment time
- Total amount (if payment is required)

Make sure all details are correct before continuing.

---

### Step 4: Complete Payment

If the selected service requires payment:

1. Choose your preferred payment method.
2. Complete the payment securely.
3. Wait for the payment confirmation.

For free services, this step is skipped automatically.

---

### Step 5: Booking Confirmation

Once your booking is successful, you'll receive a confirmation containing:

- Booking reference
- Appointment details
- Provider information
- Scheduled date and time

Your appointment will also appear in your **Bookings** dashboard.

---

## 6. Managing Your Booking

After booking an appointment, you can:

- View appointment details.
- Reschedule your appointment (when permitted).
- Cancel your booking according to the cancellation policy.
- Join online appointments if video consultations are available.
- View your booking history.

---

## 7. Need More Help?

If you encounter any issues while creating your account, logging in, verifying your email, or booking your first appointment, you can:

- Visit the FAQ section for quick answers.
- Start a Live Chat with our support team.
- Contact SlotFlow Support for additional assistance.

We're here to help you have a smooth and hassle-free experience with SlotFlow.
